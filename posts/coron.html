Crystal-clear lakes and coral-covered WWII shipwrecks make this one of the best wreck-diving destinations in Southeast Asia.

Coron is a different kind of Palawan — less about postcard beaches, more about what's underwater and hidden inland. A dozen Japanese naval vessels sunk here during World War II now sit shallow enough for open-water divers to explore, encrusted in coral and circled by reef fish.

On land, Kayangan Lake is routinely ranked among the cleanest bodies of water in the country: a short climb up a limestone ridge drops you into water so clear the boats appear to float on air. Barracuda Lake, with its unusual layered temperature changes, is a favourite among more experienced divers.

Things to do

  • Wreck diving & snorkelling
  • Kayangan & Barracuda Lakes
  • Twin Lagoon kayaking
  • Maquinit hot springs

Trip notes

Best time to visit
November to May, when visibility underwater is at its best
Getting there
Fly directly into Busuanga/Coron airport, or take a ferry from Manila or El Nido
Good for
Diving & lakes
