Beaches, waterfalls, centuries-old history, and some of the country's best regional food, all within one province.

Cebu works as a highlight reel of the Philippines in miniature. Cebu City carries the country's colonial history — Magellan's Cross and Fort San Pedro both sit within walking distance of each other — while an hour or two south, the province turns rural and dramatic.

Kawasan Falls, with its canyoneering route of cliff jumps and river slides, is the province's best-known natural attraction. Further south, Oslob and Moalboal draw visitors for whale shark encounters and sardine-run snorkelling, and the food scene — especially lechon — has a loyal following of its own.

Things to do

  • Canyoneering at Kawasan Falls
  • Historical walking tour, Cebu City
  • Whale shark watching, Oslob
  • Sardine run snorkelling, Moalboal

Trip notes

Best time to visit
