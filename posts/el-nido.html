Dramatic limestone cliffs ring turquoise lagoons that feel more like a hidden film set than a real place.

El Nido sits at the northern tip of Palawan, where karst cliffs rise straight out of the sea around a scatter of small islands. The town itself is a low-key base; the real destination is the water around it, reached by the classic boat tours labelled A through D, each covering a different cluster of lagoons, beaches, and snorkelling stops.

Big Lagoon and Small Lagoon are the headline sights, and for good reason — the light through the limestone walls turns the water an almost unnatural blue-green by midmorning. Outside the boat tours, El Nido is also a quiet base for kayaking narrow channels and simply watching the cliffs change colour at sunset.

Things to do

  • Island-hopping Tours A–D
  • Kayaking through lagoons
  • Snorkelling coral gardens
  • Beach camping on outer islands

Trip notes

Best time to visit
January to May, for calmer seas and clearer lagoon water
Getting there
Fly directly into El Nido airport, or drive roughly 5–6 hours north from Puerto Princesa
Good for
Islands & lagoons
