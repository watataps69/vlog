Two-thousand-year-old rice terraces, carved by hand into the mountains of the Cordillera, still farmed the same way today.

The Banaue Rice Terraces are often called the eighth wonder of the world, and standing at the main viewpoint it's easy to see why: tier after tier of hand-cut terracing climbs the mountainside, a living agricultural system built by the Ifugao people without machinery, over centuries.

Banaue town is the usual base for reaching the terraces and for treks to nearby Batad, whose amphitheatre-shaped terraces are even more dramatic and reachable only on foot. Local guides can also arrange visits to traditional Ifugao villages to learn about weaving, wood-carving, and the rituals tied to the rice cycle.

Things to do

  • Banaue viewpoint & terrace walks
  • Trek to Batad's amphitheatre terraces
  • Ifugao village & weaving visits
  • Tappiya Falls day hike

Trip notes

Best time to visit
November to May, avoiding the wettest months and landslide risk
Getting there
Overnight bus from Manila (roughly 9 hours) to Banaue town
Good for
Heritage & hiking
