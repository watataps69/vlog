Home to the otherworldly Chocolate Hills and the tiny, wide-eyed Philippine tarsier.

Bohol's Chocolate Hills are its signature image: well over a thousand near-identical grass-covered mounds spread across the island's interior, turning cocoa-brown in the dry season. The viewing deck at Carmen is the classic stop, best reached early to beat both the heat and the crowds.

A short drive away, the Philippine Tarsier Sanctuary protects one of the world's smallest primates in a patch of quiet forest. Round out a Bohol day with a slow lunch cruise on the Loboc River, and, along the coast, some of the calmest snorkelling and diving in the Visayas.

Things to do

  • Chocolate Hills viewpoint
  • Tarsier sanctuary visit
  • Loboc River lunch cruise
  • Snorkelling, Panglao Island

Trip notes

Best time to visit
February to April, when the hills turn their signature brown
Getting there
Fly directly into Panglao/Bohol airport, or ferry from Cebu City
Good for
Landscape & wildlife
