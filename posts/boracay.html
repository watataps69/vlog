Powder-white sand and famously calm, clear water make this the Philippines' best-known beach escape — and it still earns the reputation.

Boracay's four-kilometre White Beach is the reason most people first hear about this island, and it's every bit as fine and bright as the photos promise. The sand stays cool underfoot even at midday, and the water shelves gently, which is part of why the island became a byword for easy, no-effort beach time.

Station 1, at the northern end, has the widest stretch of sand and the best sunset views; Station 2 is the busiest, with the bulk of the restaurants and nightlife; Station 3 is quieter and better for families. Beyond the beach itself, sailing out on a traditional paraw boat at golden hour is close to a rite of passage here.

Things to do

  • Swimming & sandbar hopping
  • Island-hopping boat tours
  • Parasailing & kite-surfing
  • Sunset paraw sailing

Trip notes

Best time to visit
November to May, outside the southwest monsoon
Getting there
Fly into Caticlan (nearest) or Kalibo, then a short boat crossing to the island
Good for
Beach & watersports
