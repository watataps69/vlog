Gateway to Mount Apo, the country's highest peak, with eagle conservation and a strong local food scene close by.

Davao City is a practical, liveable base for southern Mindanao, and its main draw is what surrounds it: Mount Apo, the Philippines' highest mountain, is a multi-day trek for experienced hikers, while the Philippine Eagle Center gives a closer, easier look at one of the world's rarest raptors.

In the city itself, the People's Park and the durian and fruit stalls along Magsaysay are an easy way to spend an afternoon, and Samal Island — a short ferry ride away — adds a set of quiet white-sand beaches to round out a Davao visit.

Things to do

  • Philippine Eagle Center
  • Mount Apo trekking
  • Samal Island beach day
  • Local food & durian tasting

Trip notes

Best time to visit
March to May, for the clearest Mount Apo trekking conditions
