The country's top surf destination, with a laid-back island rhythm that extends well beyond the waves.

Cloud 9 put Siargao on the surfing map, and the island has grown into the country's most complete surf town, with breaks suited to everyone from first-timers to competition regulars. General Luna, the main hub, keeps a relaxed, cargo-shorts pace even as it's grown busier.

Away from the surf, Sugba Lagoon is worth the boat ride for a calm swim ringed by mangroves, and the Magpupungko Rock Pools — natural tide pools carved into rock — are best visited at low tide. Renting a scooter to island-hop between quieter beaches is the easiest way to see the rest.

Things to do

  • Surfing at Cloud 9
  • Sugba Lagoon day trip
  • Magpupungko Rock Pools
  • Island hopping by scooter

Trip notes

Best time to visit
August to November for the biggest swells; March–May for calmer, beginner-friendly waves
Getting there
Fly directly into Sayak Airport, Siargao
Good for
