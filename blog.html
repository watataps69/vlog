Best Place to Visit in the Philippines

Every destination we’ve explored so far, from breathtaking white-sand beaches to stunning mountain terraces carved by generations of Filipinos.

Beach & watersports

Boracay, Aklan

Powder-white sand and famously calm, clear water make this the Philippines' best-known beach escape.
Islands & lagoons

El Nido, Palawan

Dramatic limestone cliffs ring turquoise lagoons that feel more like a hidden set than a real place.
Diving & lakes

Coron, Palawan

Crystal-clear lakes, coral-covered WWII shipwrecks, and some of the best wreck diving in Southeast Asia.
Culture & waterfalls

Cebu

Beaches, waterfalls, centuries-old history, and some of the country's best regional food, all in one province.
Landscape & wildlife

Bohol

Home to the otherworldly Chocolate Hills and the tiny, wide-eyed Philippine tarsier.
Surfing

Siargao, Surigao del Norte

The country's top surf destination, with a laid-back island rhythm well beyond the waves.
Heritage & hiking

Banaue, Ifugao

Two-thousand-year-old rice terraces carved by hand into the mountains of the Cordillera.
Nature & city

Davao City

Gateway to Mount Apo, the country's highest peak, plus eagle conservation and a strong local food scene.
