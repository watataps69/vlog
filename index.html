Why this journal exists

The Philippines is more than seven thousand islands, and no single trip covers all of it. LAKBAY DIARY exists to narrow that down: short, honest write-ups of the places worth the plane ticket, what to actually do once you're there, and when to go.

Every entry is written after real research into the destination — beaches, mountains, reefs, and rice terraces alike — organised so you can compare them at a glance and plan the itinerary that fits your trip.
