About

Notes from someone still working through the list.

LAKBAY DIARY is a student travel-writing project: a small journal built to practice covering the Philippines' most-recommended destinations properly — one write-up at a time.

What this site is

This blog was built as a website-development activity: design, organise, and publish a working blog site with real navigation, multiple posts, and a layout that holds up on a phone as well as a laptop.

The content draws on an original research write-up covering ten of the most-recommended places to visit in the Philippines, expanded here into full entries with practical details — best time to visit, activities, and how to get there.

Who's writing it

LAKBAY DIARY is written and maintained by Regino Wesley as part of a website-building coursework assignment. It isn't a commercial travel service — just a practice project meant to read like one.

Have a correction, a place that deserves an entry, or a question about the site itself? Get in touch through the contact page.
